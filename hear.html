01

About the work

Guided by the question, “How do narratives affect perceptions of disability?” this piece explores my lifelong experience with hearing loss and the complexities of identity formation. Caught between the hearing and Deaf/hard of hearing communities, I illustrate the tension of navigating a world that often resists nuance—where disability can feel both invisible and defining. This work expresses the internal and external struggle to claim my identity on my own terms.
