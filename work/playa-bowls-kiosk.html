Overview

This touchscreen kiosk simplifies high-customization ordering with a clear, intuitive interface, improving speed, accessibility, and order accuracy while reducing line congestion and staff workload.

A streamlined self-service kiosk experience designed to make ordering fast, intuitive, and accessible in a high-volume food environment.

Project goals

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Create a system that is clear, flexible, and accessible
